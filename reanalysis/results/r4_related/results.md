## Method

- **Candidates:** the deep-learning coffee-disease and leaf-severity studies that `sections/01_introduction.tex` cites: ref22, ref26, ref27, ref28, ref29, yang2023ecasegformer and zhang2023lrt. Dataset papers, the UAV study (ref16) and the classical tools were not tabulated (see *Not tabulated*).
- **Selected (6 prior studies + this study):**
  - four coffee studies: ref22, ref26, ref27 and ref29;
  - one multi-crop severity-segmentation study that includes coffee leaf miner (ref28);
  - one generic transformer leaf-spot segmentation study (yang2023ecasegformer).
- **Verification:**
  - Every cell was filled from the source only.
  - Each cell lists its supporting quote(s) and URL(s).
  - `build_table.py` checks each quote verbatim against a local snapshot of the source text. Before matching, it normalizes only whitespace, ligatures and line-break hyphens.
  - Result: **75/75 quotes found verbatim** (all).
  - Independent verifier re-check: all 58 quotes for the six prior studies were found again in freshly fetched sources (all 6 sources re-opened). The 17 quotes for this study were found again in the manuscript files. See *Verifier corrections*.
- **Source access:**
  - Full text: ref22 (publisher PDF in the Incaper repository) and ref27 (open-access PDF; the original download was truncated at pp. 38–45 of 38–47; both verifiers later obtained all 10 pages, and no cell changed; see V3 and W1).
  - Abstract only: ref26, ref28, ref29 and yang2023. Their publisher pages returned HTTP 403 or a CAPTCHA, which was not bypassed.
- **n.r.** means the value is not reported in the accessible source. For the abstract-only studies it may still appear in the full text; the reason is given in each cell note.
- **Comparability caveat:** the metrics are not directly comparable across rows.
  - ref26 and ref27 report mean IoU averaged over classes. For ref27 these are background, leaf and symptom. For ref26 the classes are n.r. Background-inclusive averages are much higher than a lesion-only IoU.
  - ref29 reports pixel-level lesion Dice/IoU on field images.
  - This study reports leaf-level lesion IoU on flatbed scans.
  - Imaging conditions also differ: flatbed scans versus smartphone or field photographs.
